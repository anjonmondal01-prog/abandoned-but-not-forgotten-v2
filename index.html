Step 2 · Science & evidence

From machine to discovery

A machine story is only the start. Open any archive card to read its Evidence Chain: what the hardware did, what was measured, what the result supports, what remains uncertain, and why it matters.

  1. Find the claim. What exactly is being said about the machine or its science?
  2. Open the source. Follow the claim-specific link; check whether that page supports this exact statement.
  3. Classify the evidence. FACT / SOURCE-REPORTED means a sourced record; ESTIMATE means approximate or interpolated; INTERPRETATION is reasoning from evidence; SIMULATION is an illustrative model, not telemetry.

Unverified fields are labelled as such. A source link is a starting point, not proof that every sentence on a page has been independently checked.

      Static sketch: sunlight crosses the gap from the Sun past Earth (with its Moon) to Mars (with its two small moons, Phobos and Deimos). Sizes and distances are not to scale.

      Sunlight at Mars (computed): light from the Sun takes about 12.7 minutes to reach Mars at its average distance (1.524 AU × 8.32 minutes per AU), and the sunlight is roughly 43% as strong as at Earth (1 ÷ 1.524²). ESTIMATED from the average distance; Mars's stretched orbit makes it vary.
